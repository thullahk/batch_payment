AT Odoo 18 Community Accounting

A Comprehensive Accounting Suite for Odoo 18 Community.

Overview

This module enhances Odoo 18 Community with a complete and professional accounting suite. Manage your finances with powerful tools for full asset lifecycle management, including depreciation schedules, advanced reporting, and budget management, all within a familiar interface.

Request a Demo

Feature Highlights

Dynamic Accounting Dashboard

Get a real-time overview of your financial health. The dashboard provides key metrics and quick access to journals, customer invoices, vendor bills, and more.

Advanced Financial Reports

Generate dynamic and accurate financial statements like Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss, and Cash Flow Statements. Utilize powerful filtering and export options to analyze your company's performance.

Intuitive Bank Reconciliation

Simplify your bank reconciliation process with an easy-to-use interface. Match bank statements with accounting entries effortlessly and keep your books accurate and up-to-date.

Full Asset & Budget Control

Track the complete lifecycle of your company assets, including depreciation schedules. Create and manage budgets to monitor financial health and control spending effectively.
